## Notes

- **Music:** none, or a track you made or have a licence for. No copyrighted music.
- **Show the live app**, not slides, for every scene after the title card.
- **Say "sandbox" honestly.** Every PayPal call goes to the PayPal sandbox or the Simulator; nothing touches live PayPal. The other shops in the network and the forensics photos are made up for the demo, and the app labels them.
- **Seller quote:** use only a real quote you have permission for, the same one as in DEVPOST.md. If there is none, cut it and end on the tagline.
- **Judge mode** plays scenes 2 to 5 by itself if you'd rather record one take, then talk over it.
- Keep it under 3:00. Upload to YouTube as unlisted or public, then put the link in README.md (`[NEW VIDEO LINK]`) and DEVPOST.md.
